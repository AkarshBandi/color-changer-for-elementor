What it does

You already chose your brand colors and fonts in Elementor. WooCommerce ignores them — its buttons, prices, badges and checkout ship their own grey-and-purple palette. This plugin reads your Elementor kit and keeps WooCommerce in step with it, updating whenever you change the kit.

🎨

Connected to your kit

Brand colors and fonts follow your Elementor Site Settings automatically.

⚡

Zero configuration

Sensible defaults on activation — every element gets a color from your palette.

🎛️

Per-element control

Change any element's color from WooCommerce → Store Design, or enter a custom hex.

✨

States included

Hover, focus and disabled states derived automatically, with accessible focus outlines.

🧩

Blocks & add-ons

WooCommerce Blocks, Essential Addons and Premium Addons widgets supported.

🔌

One-click off switch

Untick "Apply my brand colours" and your store returns to normal instantly.
